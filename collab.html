Foundational Planning Context

The city is structured on **3x3 blocks forming a Sector**. Each block is a fundamental unit with a 340m x 340m grid, accommodating 1700-2000 residents. The blocks contain small supermarkets, shops, offices, apartments, or bungalows. The blocks are categorized into four types of housing (Type 1 to Type 4) based on square footage (e.g., 700 sq ft to 1200 sq ft).

This grid system is the basis for the following algorithmic solutions, ensuring that every placement and route is computationally optimized.
