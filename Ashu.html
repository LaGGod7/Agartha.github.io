Comprehensive Sustainable City Initiatives 🏙️

Hi, I am Ashutosh. This document outlines the full scope of business cases developed for Agartha — a new, self-sufficient, and sustainable city with a population of 4 million, located between Pune and Nashik near the banks of the Pravara River. These initiatives are designed to support the city's long-term growth, improve quality of life, and align with global Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Business Cases:

  1. **Allocation of Funds to Industries**
  2. **Electricity Substation Monitoring System**
  3. **Theatre Allocation and Management System**
  4. **Integrated Tax Management System**
  5. **City-wide Hygiene and Sanitation Monitoring**
  6. **Municipal Vehicle Allocation and Optimization (CO)**
  7. **Sector-wise Construction Planning and Coordination (CO)**
  8. **Crime Management and Police Workforce Optimization**
  9. **Integrated Tourism Promotion and Visitor Services**
  10. **Government Schemes Awareness and Beneficiary Tracking System**

These initiatives form the blueprint for a modern, sustainable, efficient, and inclusive urban ecosystem for the future of Agartha.
