Detailed Initiatives

1. Allocation of Funds to Industries

Policy: revenue checks, bonus & loan rules

Fund allocation enforces revenue-based rules: 200% of annual revenue if revenue > ₹5,00,000, otherwise ₹5,00,000 base. Small-scale industries get +15% bonus. Loans offered when requested. Uses priority scoring for fair disbursement.

2. Electricity Substation Monitoring System

Realtime telemetry • predictive maintenance

Sensors stream voltage/current/temperature. Segment tree (over substations) + RB-tree for predicted failures and a min-heap for alarms ensures fast regional aggregation and prioritized maintenance scheduling.

3. Theatre Allocation and Management

Venue caps: 2×2.5k, 8k, 15k

Interval-based scheduling with venue preferences. Classical events receive discounted allocation priority. If preferred slot busy, system tries alternate venues or suggests time shifts.

4. Integrated Tax Management System

Incentives to attract companies

Unified portal, skip-list / ordered indexes, Fenwick for monthly aggregates, and tax-rate lookup tables. Lower road & capital-gain taxes used as incentives to encourage company formation.

5. City-wide Hygiene & Sanitation Monitoring

Citizen reports • smart-bin optimisations

Grid-hash buckets + skiplist for urgency-ordered tasks, sensor-driven smart-bin pickups, and Fenwick for daily operational KPIs help keep the city clean efficiently.

6. Municipal Vehicle Allocation & Optimization

Routing (Dijkstra/A*), VRP heuristics

Fleet inventory in HashMap, route costs via Dijkstra/A*, and VRP heuristics + heaps for dispatch. EVs assigned to routes with charging windows to reduce emissions.

7. Sector-wise Construction Planning & Coordination

Sector = 8 residential blocks + center block

DAG of tasks + topological sort to enforce dependencies, and per-block segment trees for occupancy scheduling; automated conflict detection avoids simultaneous high-impact works.

8. Crime Management & Police Workforce Optimization

Heatmap ranking • shift matching

Fenwick for time-windowed incident counts, AVL/skiplist for ranked hotspot lists, and bipartite-matching (Hungarian/max-flow) for officer↔shift optimization; Dijkstra for dispatch routing.
