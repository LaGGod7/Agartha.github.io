About Our Team & Project 💡

The Agartha Initiative

We are a passionate team of computer science enthusiasts working together on a groundbreaking project: the **Building of City Agartha**. Our goal is to apply advanced **Design and Algorithm Analysis (DAA)** techniques to develop sustainable, data-driven solutions for modern urban challenges, such as optimized resource allocation, traffic flow, and infrastructure planning.

Our goal is to integrate technology and innovation to create systems that are scalable, efficient, and user-friendly, demonstrating the practical application of theoretical computer science in real-world infrastructure.

Project Insights & Analysis 📊

Conceptual Map of Agartha

This hand-drawn map forms the foundation of our project. It utilizes a grid-based system for city planning, which is essential for applying **Graph Algorithms** (like Shortest Path or Minimum Spanning Tree) to optimize infrastructure like road networks, resource pipelines, and emergency routes.
