Detailed Initiatives

1. Air Traffic Control System

✈️
SDG 9.1 & SDG 11.2

Manages civilian, cargo, and emergency air traffic using conflict-free scheduling.

Input: Aircraft list A with position, speed, altitude
Sort A by time-to-intersection
For each aircraft i:
  For each aircraft j > i:
    if distance(i,j) < safety_threshold:
       adjust altitude or delay time
Use priority queue for emergency flights
Time Complexity: O(N log N)

2. Air Quality Index Monitoring

🌬️
SDG 3.9 & SDG 11.6

Real-time AQI computation with automated response triggers.

Sensors collect PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2
For each zone:
  AQI = max(subIndex(pollutant))
If AQI > threshold:
  restrict industries
  alert hospitals
Time Complexity: O(N)

3. Cement Production & Emission Control

🏭
SDG 9.4 & SDG 12.2

Balances cement demand with emission ceilings.

Input: Demand D, Emission Limit E
For each batch:
  calculate emission e
  if total_e + e <= E:
     approve batch
  else:
     delay or reduce batch
Greedy optimization
O(N)

4. Farm Land & Solar Allocation

🌾
SDG 7.2 & SDG 15.1

Optimizes land usage between agriculture and renewable energy.

For each land parcel:
  compute soil_score
  compute solar_score
If soil_score > solar_score:
  allocate farming
Else:
  allocate solar
Dynamic programming
O(N)

5. Small Scale Industries Enablement

🏪
SDG 8.3 & SDG 9.3

Cluster-based growth of MSMEs.

Cluster industries by resource need
Allocate power, water, logistics
Monitor output & employment
Graph clustering
O(V + E)

6. National Park & Wildlife Maintenance

🌳
SDG 15.1 & SDG 11.4

Ensures biodiversity conservation and sustainable tourism.

Track animal population using sensor data
If population drops below threshold:
  restrict human access
  increase ranger patrol
Allocate resources using priority queues
Time Complexity: O(N log N)

7. Tech Park Space Allocation

💻
SDG 9.5 & SDG 8.2

Optimized space allocation for startups and tech enterprises.

Input: Company size, growth rate, resource needs
Sort companies by growth score
Allocate office space accordingly
Rebalance quarterly
Greedy + sorting
O(N log N)
